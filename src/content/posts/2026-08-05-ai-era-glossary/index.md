18 months ago, half of these words didn't exist. Now they run my daily workflow, and one is already in 60,000+ repos.

A field guide to the new developer vocabulary:

- **Token.** The unit AI reads and writes in. 1,000 tokens is about 750 words. Cost and context limits are counted here, not in words.
- **Context window.** How much a model can hold at once. Frontier models now reach 1M tokens. Your prompt, files and history all share that budget.
- **CLAUDE.md.** A README written for your AI, not your team. Project rules and commands, auto-loaded every session.
- **AGENTS.md.** The same idea, vendor-neutral. One file any coding agent can read. Already in 60,000+ projects, now stewarded by the Linux Foundation.
- **Skills (SKILL.md).** Reusable expertise the model loads only when the task calls for it. A huge skill costs almost nothing until it's needed.
- **Agent Mode.** When the AI stops answering and starts doing: plan, edit, run, fix, looping across your files.
- **MCP.** The open "USB-C for AI tools". One protocol to connect agents to your data.

The frameworks will keep changing. Speaking the language is the skill that lasts.
